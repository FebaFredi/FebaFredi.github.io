1:00 PM · Setup Begins
Venue Preparation
Ever After Beach Weddings sets up gazebo, chairs, aisle decor and sound at Point Dume. Chronos Media sets up audio and livestream equipment.

Two Cadillac Escalades arrive at Embassy Suites — one for Stephen, one for Feba.
3:00 PM · Getting Ready
Vazquez Photography Begins
Kylie Vazquez (@kylievazquezphoto) starts at 3:00 PM. Captures getting ready photos at Embassy Suites, then photographs Stephen and Feba leaving separately in their Escalades.
3:15 PM · Guest Transport
Party Bus from Embassy Suites
Party bus picks up wedding guests from Embassy Suites and brings them to Point Dume for the ceremony.
4:00 – 5:30 PM · Ceremony
Elopement Ceremony
Oceanfront ceremony at Point Dume with up to 70 guests. Officiated by Pastor Mathew D George. Chronos Media handles audio, livestreaming, and additional photography coverage. VJ Drones hired for drone shots of the ceremony and entrance.
5:30 – 6:30 PM · Photos
Family Photos
Vazquez Photography and Chronos Media capture family photos, couple portraits, and bridal party shots. VJ Drones provides aerial coverage.
6:30 PM · The Exit
Blue BMW M4 · Ride Into the Sunset
Stephen and Feba hop into a blue BMW M4 and drive off into the sunset. The ultimate send-off.
7:00 PM · Reception
Fogo De Chão
Feba changes into a blue Indian attire for the reception. Dinner for ~70 guests. MC'd by Vinu Joseph. Chronos Media handles audio, livestreaming, photography, and photo booth.

DoubleTree Irvine
Stephen & Feba stay together at DoubleTree Irvine for the next two days.
